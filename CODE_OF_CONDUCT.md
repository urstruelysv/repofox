# Code of Conduct

RepoFox is committed to being a respectful, constructive project for users, contributors, and maintainers.

## Our Standard

Examples of behavior that help this project:

- being kind, direct, and professional
- assuming good intent while still giving honest feedback
- focusing discussions on the code, product, and user impact
- accepting correction gracefully
- helping newer contributors get oriented

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or conduct
- bad-faith trolling or deliberate disruption
- publishing private information without permission
- hostile review behavior meant to embarrass rather than improve the work

## Scope

This applies in GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, docs, demos, and other project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email the maintainers at `founders@repofox.dev`.

Please include:

- what happened
- where it happened
- any relevant links or screenshots
- how to contact you for follow-up

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or participation that do not align with this code of conduct.

Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.
